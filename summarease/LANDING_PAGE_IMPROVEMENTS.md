   - New title: "Sat-Brain: AI Note-Taking & Study App | Free Month Trial"

   - **Impact**: Primary SEO ranking factor
   - **Time**: 30 mins

4. **Fix broken footer links (privacy, terms, status)**
   - Create /privacy and /terms pages (legal requirement)
   - Fix or remove status link
   - **Risk**: Legal compliance issue
   - **Time**: 3-4 hours (with legal content)

5. **Extract 290 lines of inline CSS to external file**
   - Move from component to .css file
   - Improves bundle size and caching
   - **Impact**: 20-30% faster initial render
   - **Time**: 1 hour

## 💡 Pro Tips

1. **Focus on data first**: Install GA4 before optimizing anything else
2. **Test with real users**: Show landing page to 5 potential customers for feedback
3. **Measure everything**: Before/after metrics for each change
4. **Don't fake social proof**: Better to show "500 beta users" than fake "10,000 users"
5. **Mobile-first**: 60%+ of traffic will be mobile
6. **Page speed matters**: Every 100ms delay = 1% conversion loss
7. **A/B test headlines**: Highest ROI optimization
8. **Build email list early**: Even before launch

## 🚨 Common Pitfalls to Avoid

❌ **Don't**: Use fake testimonials or inflated numbers  
✅ **Do**: Show honest beta user feedback or "early adopter" messaging

❌ **Don't**: Optimize for keywords at expense of clarity  
✅ **Do**: Write for humans first, optimize for search second

❌ **Don't**: Add every feature/animation because it's cool  
✅ **Do**: Test impact on conversion before keeping it

❌ **Don't**: Copy competitor landing pages exactly  
✅ **Do**: Learn from them but maintain unique voice

❌ **Don't**: Deploy all changes at once  
✅ **Do**: Incremental improvements with before/after measurement
