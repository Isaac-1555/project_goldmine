Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 5, 2025
This is a plain-language summary for convenience and not legal advice. If you have questions, contact support@sat-brain.app.

Sat-Brain ("we", "our", "us") provides tools that help students summarize documents, ask questions, and generate study visuals.

Information We Collect

  • Account: email, name (if provided).
  • Content you upload: documents (e.g., PDFs, DOCX) and queries.
  • Usage: device/browser info, pages viewed, interactions, diagnostics.
  • Cookies: for session management and analytics.

How We Use Information

  • Provide, maintain, and improve features (summaries, Q&A, visualizations).
  • Personalize your experience (recent files, preferences, theme).
  • Communicate about updates, security, and support.
  • Prevent abuse, fraud, and misuse.

AI & Third-Party Services

We may process uploaded content with AI providers to generate outputs (e.g., summaries, diagrams). We use cloud services for hosting, storage, and analytics. Providers are restricted to using your data solely to deliver services to us.

Data Retention & Deletion

  • Uploaded files and outputs are retained until you delete them or close your account.
  • You may request deletion of your account and associated content at any time.

Security

We use industry-standard security measures. No method is 100% secure.

Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or export your data. To exercise rights, contact support@sat-brain.app.

Children’s Privacy

Sat-Brain is intended for users 13+ (or the age of digital consent in your region).

International Transfers

Your data may be processed outside your country. We implement appropriate safeguards.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will update the “Last updated” date above.

Questions? Contact support@sat-brain.app.
